About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It started in 1979 as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science.

In 2001, it was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM). On 1 February 2007, it was officially given full university status and became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.
